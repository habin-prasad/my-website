### Situation
Engineering teams treated QA as a downstream bottleneck at the end of sprint cycles. Defect detection occurred late in staging environments, leading to high rework costs and unpredictable release schedules.

### Task
Transform organizational culture from passive QA testing to an integrated Quality Engineering model, establishing shared ownership of quality across product managers, developers, and SDETs.

### Action
* **Quality Gates in CI/CD**: Standardized mandatory PR quality checks including code coverage thresholding, static analysis, and fast contract tests.
* **Team Scaling & Hiring**: Hired, structured, and managed a high-performing automation team, establishing career growth paths and peer code review standards.
* **DORA Metrics Dashboards**: Built real-time analytics tracking production escape rates, change failure rates, and lead time for changes.

### Result
* Reduced production severity-1 incidents by **80% over 12 months**.
* Increased deployment frequency from bi-weekly releases to multiple daily releases.
