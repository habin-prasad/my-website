### Situation
The organization suffered from a traditional "siloed QA" culture where manual testers validated software late in sprint cycles. This produced delayed feedback, high attrition, low engineering trust, and erratic release cadences.

### Task
Define a multi-year Quality Engineering vision, establish clear technical career matrices, upskill the existing team, and embed quality engineering practices directly into squad sprint ownership.

### Action
* **SDET Career Matrix Definition**: Authored a 5-tier technical progression path (Junior SDET to Principal SDET), aligning engineering expectations around software design, system architecture, and infrastructure.
* **Upskilling & Hiring**: Conducted internal bootcamps on TypeScript, Go, Docker, and CI/CD pipelines, while directly hiring senior SDET talent to pair with squads.
* **DORA Metrics Accountability**: Partnered with VPs of Engineering to establish real-time tracking for Lead Time for Changes, Change Failure Rate, and Mean Time to Recovery (MTTR).

### Result
* Successfully transitioned **22 manual QA engineers into full-stack SDET roles**.
* Reduced average release lead time from **14 days to 4 hours**.
